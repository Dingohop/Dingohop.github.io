About

Most of the work is invisible on purpose.

I'm Michael Taylor. I edit video from the East Coast for creators, churches, podcasts and small businesses, turning raw footage into something ready to publish.

The approach is the same every time: work out what the video is actually for, cut what gets in the way, and shape the pacing so the point lands. The dead air you never sat through, the pause that got tightened, the audio you never noticed was fixed — that's the job.
