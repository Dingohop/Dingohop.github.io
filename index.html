Services

The kind of work I do.

01 / Short-Form

Reels, Shorts and TikToks

These are usually cut down from footage you already have, and the idea is to build the clip around the one thing you want people to walk away with.

02 / Long-Form

YouTube, podcasts and interviews

Most of the work here is pacing. A good hour of content shouldn't feel like an hour, so I spend the time tightening it up.

03 / Repurposing

One recording, several cuts

Most long recordings have a few really good moments buried in them. I go through and pull those out so they can stand on their own.

About

The best editing is the part you don't notice.

My name is Michael Taylor and I'm a video editor based on the East Coast. I work with creators, churches, podcasts and small businesses, taking whatever footage they have and turning it into something they can actually post.

Every project pretty much starts the same way. I figure out what the video is supposed to do first, and then I cut whatever is getting in the way of that. After that it's mostly a question of pacing.

If I've done my job right you shouldn't really notice any of it. You won't sit through the dead air because it isn't there anymore, and you won't hear the room noise underneath the audio. That's honestly the whole job.
